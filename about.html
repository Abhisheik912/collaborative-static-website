Our Story

AutoStyle was founded in 2015 by a group of automotive enthusiasts who believed every car deserves a personal touch. What started as a small workshop in Chennai has grown into one of South India's most trusted car decor studios.

We combine craftsmanship, premium materials, and a deep passion for automobiles to deliver transformations that last. Every project we take on is treated as a work of art — because to us, your car is more than transport. It's an extension of who you are.
